To start off I really didn't have high expectations for
[Six Flags Magic Mountain](https://www.sixflags.com/magicmountain) . I'm not really a fan of theme
parks or I guess the ones that I've been to which are
[Disney Land](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/) and
[Knott's Berry Farm](https://www.sixflags.com/knotts). I've found that the lines were too long, and
the rides not cool enough to justify the absurdly long lines. I came into Six Flags expecting a
similar experience but I was pleasantly surprised. Not only were the lines were generally reasonable
and the rides were all pretty good. I went with 5 other friends and spent the time going on just
roller coasters so my review will surround these experiences specifically.

## The Best Rides

### Tatsu

Tatsu is the ride that sticks the most in my mind. The ride is unique in that it unlike most rides
the ride from the get go puts you into an upside down position. meaning that all you see is the
ground as you go up. It's extremely exhilarating when finally drop because you are forced to look
down as you move. The ride also incorporates multiple loops which leads to a truly amazing
experience at the top of the loop given your position. The ride is one of the longer ones as well
with many twists and turns. The only issue is that this rides does have the longest line of all the
rides we tried so waiting until later would probably be a good idea.

### Wonder Woman

The uniqueness of this ride comes from how the ride is a single seater where there is no one next to
you. There are people ahead and behind you but the single seater experience was surprisingly
something I hadn't experienced before. I didn't how important it was knowing someone was next to you
when it came to feeling fear. Furthermore, the ride has one massive and quite steep drop at the very
beginning which lead to massive acceleration making it as fun as Tatsu. It had a mix of more drops
and a couple of corkscrews that added to the experience and since you're by yourself I actually felt
a bit anxious during this ride. It feels really fast and because of the constant drops there's this
constant up and down that other might not find as fun.

### Viper

Viper had the highest drop out of all the rides we went on. Luckily the drop is not as steep as
wonder woman but because it was so high there was like a good maybe 30 seconds - 1 minute that was
just going up slowly. This slow ascent built a lot of anticipation and the designers of this ride
really know how to play with emotions because as you reach the top there is slight unexpected delay
for when you drop that throws off anyone's preparations and makes the ensuring ride exiting. The
rest of the ride and its main appeal is that its pretty much all loops and a somewhat tight turn a
later into the ride which made the ride really fun and unique even compared to the other rides we
went on. However, Viper is the only ride where I felt a slight bit of nausea after it was over.

## Other Areas

While the rides are the most important part of theme parks its not the only things that makes theme
parks a nicer experience. On thing I would like to reiterate is that I found the park to be just
overall less crowded than compared to other parks and that generally made everything feel more fluid
throughout the day.

A decently large factor for anyone is still going to be the price and it is honestly less expensive
than the other theme parks I have gone to with the base ticket price being just 55-65\$ if bought
online. The parking is 40\$ but that's normal for pretty much all theme parks. The major value
though the All Day Dining add on which is 35\$ and allows you to get a free meal every 90 minutes
which is absurdly nice if you plan to spend the entire day at Six Flags. A few of my friends had it
and it was incredibly nice to be able to eat pretty much whenever we wanted. According to some of my
friends the food was actually better than other theme parks overall.

## Final Review

I would rate Six Flags the best theme park out of all of ones I've gone to so far and I strongly
recommend going. I don't recommend bringing really small children though due to many rides probably
being off limits. Slight tidbit that while there is security at the entrance my entire friend group
was able to get in without getting our bags checked so if you want to risk it you can try to just
bring in a bunch of outside food. If you don't want to risk it I recommend buying the All Day Dining
if you plan to spend the entire day at Six Flags.
